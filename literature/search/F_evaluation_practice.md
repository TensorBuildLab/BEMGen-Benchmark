## Synthesis (counts over the 15 rows 1-14b)

Public test sets: 1 clearly (AECBench, a knowledge benchmark, not model generation); 1 partial (paper 6, example subsets). No EnergyPlus-generation paper (1-4, 7, 8) released its full test set or reported one. Reference comparison: papers 1 (ground-truth IDF plus simulated results), 3 and 2 (official iUnit model, error-free check), 9 (manually checked tasks) and 12 (expert rubrics) compare to a reference; 4 and 11 use simulation or ground truth that I could not verify. Numeric tolerances: 0 of 15; paper 1 demands exact match, and only BEMEval (added) mentions thresholds, as future work. Variance: 2 (papers 2 and 3, the same 10-run iUnit experiment at temperature 0); none report confidence intervals or multiple seeds. More than 10 cases: 6 verified (1: 152; 6: 648; 9: 100 per language; 11: 173; 12: 4,800; 14a: 110) plus paper 4 (402, slides only). Headline scores are mostly self-reported 97-100% on in-distribution, mostly single-building or simple-geometry tasks, with no shared test set and no cross-paper comparability. Papers 7, 8, 13 were unreadable and are scored "not found".
